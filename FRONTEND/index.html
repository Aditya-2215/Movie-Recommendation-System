UNDER THE HOOD

Simple interface. Real machine learning.

CineMatch uses a content-based recommendation approach. Movie information is transformed into TF-IDF vectors and compared using cosine similarity.

TMDB provides the visual and descriptive information that makes the recommendation experience feel like a complete movie platform.
